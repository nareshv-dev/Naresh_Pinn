# Qualitative AI-Style Audit of the Revised PINN Manuscript

## What linguistic features may make the manuscript feel machine-polished?

### Takeaway
The manuscript is technically specific and materially better than generic AI-written academic prose, but several passages are unusually uniform, compressed, and exhaustively enumerated. The main concern is not individual vocabulary; it is the repeated pattern of claim, qualification, three-part list, and scope disclaimer across nearly every section.

### Cited Findings
- **Abstract — moderate risk.** The opening two sentences are distinctive because they describe a concrete defect in the authors' earlier architecture: separate stress outputs could disagree with the concentration field that should determine them. This is strong author-specific material. The remainder becomes dense and list-like: inputs, residuals, reference simulator, split policy, three baselines, three budgets, three seeds, five evaluation categories, and four excluded claims all appear in one short abstract. The information is useful, but the nearly complete checklist structure can read as machine-compressed. Consider retaining the defect, remedy, evaluation setup, and scope while dropping one layer of enumeration. [Manuscript, abstract](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L79)
- **Introduction — moderate risk.** The first three paragraphs are concrete and logically motivated. The final paragraph switches to an explicit four-item sequence: “First… Second… Third… Fourth…,” followed immediately by another four-category evaluation list and a final limitation. This is clear but strongly formulaic. A more natural version could group the related choices into two sentences and explain which decision arose from which observed problem. [Manuscript, introduction](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L100)
- **Related work — moderate-to-high risk.** The electrochemical and thermal subsections use a repeated catalogue pattern: author + method + contrast with this study. The prose is accurate in form, but nearly every citation receives one balanced sentence and the section ends with a neat gap statement. Table I already performs this comparison, so the prose can sound generated from the table. Reduce the roll call and spend more words on the two or three studies that actually affected the architecture or experiment. [Manuscript, related work](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L109)
- **“Why mechanics remains in the model” — low risk and worth preserving.** The distinction between a discharge-scale stress indicator and a cycle-level degradation target is specific to this project. The sentence explaining that adding one degradation equation would not validate aging is direct, cautious, and technically meaningful. It sounds like an author responding to a real design question rather than filling a literature-review template. [Manuscript, mechanics rationale](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L116)
- **Governing physics — low stylistic risk.** The text is conventional because it introduces standard equations and symbols. Phrases such as “denotes,” “is completed by,” and “we therefore use” are normal mathematical exposition. Rephrasing equations or established definitions merely to change detector output would reduce clarity and could introduce mistakes. The valuable qualifiers—stress is calculated rather than measured, and crack growth is outside the model—should remain. [Manuscript, governing physics](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L155)
- **Architecture and residuals — low-to-moderate risk.** The architectural facts are concrete: two four-layer, 96-unit tanh subnetworks; radius only in the concentration branch; fixed 7200 s reference time; and hard initial conditions. These details anchor the prose. The residual descriptions are necessarily formal. The most generic sentences are those that simply restate what each displayed equation already shows; they can be shortened if space is needed. [Manuscript, proposed PINN](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L197)
- **Supervised loss and implementation sequence — mixed risk.** The explanation of why removing stress targets does not remove mechanical evaluation is strong because it separates magnitude accuracy from internal consistency. By contrast, “The data pass through five steps” introduces a smooth, textbook-like procedural sequence whose sentences have similar length and syntax. The centre-integration warning is excellent author-specific detail; it describes an implementation choice and its consequence. Preserve that detail and make the surrounding workflow less ceremonial. [Manuscript, loss and implementation](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L244)
- **Experimental methodology — low-to-moderate risk.** The numerical setup is sufficiently specific to sound authentic. The explanation of why a random row split leaks near-duplicate trajectory information is one of the strongest passages in the paper. The three-model description and the three optional ablations are compact, evenly balanced lists; they may read as too polished, but they are defensible if these experiments are actually implemented. [Manuscript, experimental methodology](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L263)
- **Editing defect — high priority.** “Evaluation measures” appears twice consecutively. This is a visible production error and can undermine confidence more than any style score. Remove one of the two subsection commands. [Duplicate headings](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L315)
- **Evaluation measures — moderate-to-high risk.** The section announces exactly nine measures and then lists all nine in one long sentence. It follows with paired explanations of why MAPE is unsuitable and why temperature rise is preferred. This exhaustive accounting is useful for a protocol, but the density and symmetry resemble generated academic prose. A small grouped table—prediction accuracy, physical consistency, and efficiency—would improve readability without obscuring the measures. [Manuscript, evaluation measures](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L317)
- **Aggregation and acceptance rule — moderate risk.** The section is commendably predeclared, but phrases such as “accepted only if,” “not sufficient evidence on its own,” and “valid only for” repeat the paper's broader claim-limiting pattern. The content should remain, but one concise decision rule and one timing condition would sound less defensive. [Manuscript, aggregation](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L357)
- **Results and discussion — high risk of sounding provisional rather than authored from completed research.** The section openly says that all result values are pending and then provides “interpretation planned before seeing the numbers.” This honesty is preferable to invented results, but it makes the paper read like a protocol or generated manuscript shell rather than a finished conference paper. No stylistic rewrite can solve that. The main remedy is to run the experiment, insert the results, and discuss at least one observed success, failure, or unexpected tradeoff in concrete terms. [Manuscript, results](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L362)
- **Limitations — low-to-moderate risk.** The limitations are technically precise and appropriately narrow. However, the section accumulates exclusions in repeated list form: laboratory validation, spatial scales, material behavior, current profiles, degradation mechanisms, numerical effects, and optimization issues. This produces a uniformly cautious voice. Consolidate related exclusions and retain the most consequential ones. [Manuscript, limitations](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L394)
- **“Reading failed runs” — low risk and worth preserving.** The diagnostic links between residual patterns and likely implementation errors are unusually concrete. They communicate practical experience with the model. The four consecutive “X suggests Y” sentences are somewhat parallel, but the specificity outweighs that stylistic concern. [Manuscript, failed-run diagnosis](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L408)
- **Conclusion — moderate-to-high risk.** It closely repeats the abstract and introduction: earlier four-output model, revised concentration/temperature network, elastic stress calculation, physics terms, condition holdout, three baselines, and cautions about consistency. Repetition is normal, but here the ordering is almost identical. A stronger conclusion should foreground whatever the completed experiment actually revealed and then state one limitation that changes how the result should be used. [Manuscript, conclusion](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L414)
- **Data availability and acknowledgment — low concern.** Both are conventional and necessarily generic. The data statement will become more credible if it names a repository or archive and the exact release contents. The acknowledgment is concise and should not be expanded merely to sound different. [Manuscript, end matter](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L419)

### Inferences
- The passages most likely to attract suspicion are those that package many design choices into perfectly balanced lists. They are not technically wrong; their rhythm is simply more uniform than normal project writing.
- The strongest evidence of genuine authorship is found where the text records a discovered defect, a concrete implementation decision, or a diagnostic lesson: independent stress heads drifting from concentration, condition-level splitting, the centre limit in radial integration, and interpretations of failed runs.
- Completing the experiment will improve the paper's authorship signal more than synonym substitution. Real results create naturally irregular discussion: one metric improves, another does not, one condition is harder, and one failure requires explanation.

### Gaps
- This audit cannot determine how a proprietary detector will classify the manuscript or assign a defensible AI-writing percentage.
- The manuscript does not yet contain completed numerical results, so the most naturally author-specific portion of a research paper—discussion of observed evidence—cannot be assessed.

## Which passages should be preserved because they reflect the authors' actual reasoning?

### Takeaway
Several passages already sound grounded in hands-on model development. Revision should protect these details and use them as the stylistic model for the rest of the paper.

### Cited Findings
- The opening account of the earlier model's flaw gives a specific before-and-after design reason. It states what was wrong, why it mattered physically, and how the new architecture addresses it. [Abstract](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L79)
- The explanation that a smooth stress curve can fit labels while remaining inconsistent with its own concentration branch is a precise statement of the research problem. [Introduction](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L104)
- The argument for retaining mechanics and postponing degradation correctly ties claims to available data: single-discharge trajectories can support stress reconstruction, while aging claims need repeated cycles and health-related observations. [Mechanics rationale](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L116)
- The warning against trajectory-row random splitting shows understanding of correlated simulation data and explains why full operating conditions are held out. [Data split rationale](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L267)
- The description of sorting concentration by radius, using cumulative trapezoidal integration, and applying the analytic centre limit identifies a real numerical issue that can shift the stress peak. [Implementation sequence](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L256)
- The manuscript explicitly refuses to infer missing metrics from a short run or an expected trend. This is valuable research integrity and should remain, although a submitted conference paper ultimately needs completed results. [Results status](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L363)
- The failed-run interpretation connects particular error patterns with boundary conditions, scaling, elastic parameters, quadrature, and thermal inputs. This practical troubleshooting knowledge is distinctive. [Failure analysis](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L408)

### Inferences
- The rest of the manuscript would feel more natural if it adopted the same pattern as these passages: identify an observed problem, describe the decision made, and state the consequence or remaining uncertainty.
- First-person plural is not itself a detector risk here. “We” is strongest when attached to an action the authors actually performed; it is weaker when used only to announce a list or restate a section's contents.

### Gaps
- The paper does not yet include experiment-specific observations such as which seed failed, which held-out temperature was harder, or where peak-stress error occurred. Those details should come only from completed outputs.

## What revisions would improve authenticity and readability without targeting detector evasion?

### Takeaway
Revise for scientific clarity: remove duplicated structure, reduce catalogue prose, connect choices to observed project problems, and replace the placeholder results section with evidence once the runs finish. These changes may also reduce formulaic signals, but no detector outcome can be promised.

### Cited Findings
- **Fix immediately:** delete the duplicated `\\subsection{Evaluation measures}` command. [Duplicate headings](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L315)
- **Abstract:** keep the original-model defect, the concentration-derived stress remedy, the condition-level split, and the scope boundary. Compress the lists of residuals, baselines, budgets, seeds, and metrics into fewer clauses. [Abstract](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L79)
- **Introduction:** replace “First/Second/Third/Fourth” with a narrative that connects each change to a reason: operating inputs provide one conditional surrogate; deleting the stress heads enforces coupling; condition-level splits prevent trajectory leakage; matched labels make the model comparison fair. [Introduction](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L100)
- **Related work:** retain a short synthesis in prose and let Table I carry the ten-study inventory. Discuss the closest electrochemical surrogate, the closest multiphysics work, and the reason neither answers the paper's concentration-to-stress consistency question. [Related work and Table I](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L109)
- **Methods:** keep exact values and implementation decisions. Replace procedural framing such as “five steps” with two or three paragraphs organized around data preparation, optimization, and stress calculation. [Implementation sequence](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L256)
- **Metrics:** group the nine quantities by purpose in a compact table or three short sentences: predictive accuracy, physical consistency, and computational cost. Preserve the justification for avoiding stress MAPE. [Evaluation measures](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L317)
- **Results:** do not submit the placeholder table as a completed research result. After the full runs, report the actual direction and magnitude of change, variation across seeds, worse held-out condition, and any tradeoff. If results are unavailable, present the document explicitly as a protocol or work-in-progress rather than a completed empirical paper. [Results and Discussion](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L362)
- **Limitations:** combine overlapping scope exclusions, then retain the limitations that materially affect interpretation: simulation-only validation, uncertain elastic parameters, single-discharge constant-current scope, and absence of degradation data. [Limitations](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L394)
- **Conclusion:** rewrite only after results are available. Lead with the observed comparison rather than repeating the architecture. State whether consistency improved, what happened to stress accuracy, and which held-out condition exposed the main weakness. [Conclusion](E:/Naresh_Projects/PINN_EV_battery/deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.tex#L414)

### Inferences
- Synonym replacement, random sentence-length variation, deliberate grammar mistakes, or paraphrasing standard equations would not improve the research and could damage accuracy.
- A detector-resilient claim cannot be validated by this audit. The defensible target is a manuscript that accurately reflects the authors' decisions, data, and observed results.
- Similarity and AI-writing checks answer different questions. Rewriting for natural authorship will not remove legitimate similarity from equations, standard method names, references, or a repository copy of the authors' earlier submission.

### Gaps
- No external AI-detection service was run. Such services cannot provide a dependable ground truth for authorship, and their percentages are not interchangeable with Turnitin's indicator.
- No source-overlap analysis is included in this note; plagiarism/similarity requires a separate comparison against published and institutional sources.
