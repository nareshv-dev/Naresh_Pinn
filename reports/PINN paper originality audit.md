# Independent Checks Clarify Originality Risks

Yes, other tools can help screen the revised PINN EV battery paper, but none can reproduce Turnitin's result unless it has the same private student-paper repository, publication collection, assignment settings, and exclusions. The independent audit performed here gives a useful diagnosis rather than a substitute score. **Fourteen searches of distinctive sentences found no credible exact match on the public web**, which makes copied public prose unlikely in the sampled passages. The more important issue is local self-overlap: an exact-sequence comparison with the earlier submitted manuscript found that **30.46% of the revised body participates in matching runs of at least 12 words**. That figure is a local coverage measurement, not a plagiarism percentage or a prediction of Turnitin. Most retained text sits in equations, technical explanations, tables, metric definitions, and the experimental protocol. The revised paper also contains several authentic project-specific passages, although balanced lists, uniformly compressed prose, and a results section whose values remain pending can still make the document feel machine-polished. A fresh institutional report remains necessary, and the earlier repository submission should be handled explicitly rather than hidden through superficial paraphrasing.

## Other tools provide screening, not Turnitin equivalence

Public plagiarism services, search engines, publisher similarity systems, and local text-comparison programs can each find some kinds of overlap. AI-writing detectors can also flag linguistic patterns. **Their outputs are not interchangeable**, because each service uses a different corpus, matching threshold, document parser, and classification model. Turnitin compares a submission with current and archived web pages, publications, and previously submitted student papers; an ordinary web checker cannot see that private repository ([Turnitin similarity-score guidance](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/23435833938701-Understanding-the-similarity-score)). Turnitin itself states that similarity is matching text rather than a determination of plagiarism, so even its percentage requires source-by-source academic judgment ([Turnitin and plagiarism](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/34400565079053-Turnitin-and-plagiarism)).

The same caution applies more strongly to AI detection. A detector can classify writing patterns, but it cannot establish who wrote a passage. Turnitin acknowledges that its model can misidentify human-written, AI-generated, and AI-paraphrased text and says the indicator should not be the sole basis for adverse action ([Turnitin AI Writing Report guidance](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report)). Therefore, another app can provide a second opinion, but **no responsible check can certify “0% AI,” prove human authorship, or guarantee the score that Turnitin will display**.

## Public-web probes find no suspicious prose match

The revised manuscript was tested with fourteen exact-phrase probes drawn from the title, abstract, introduction, related work, architecture, evaluation, results, limitations, and conclusion. Searches included distinctive project-specific language about independent stress outputs disagreeing with concentration, avoiding a trajectory-specific end time, scoring temperature rise above ambient, and refusing to estimate missing metrics. **None produced a credible exact match to another public document.** The detailed queries and section-by-section findings are preserved in the [public-web audit](../research_notes/PINN%20paper%20originality%20audit/web_similarity.md).

This finding supports a narrow conclusion: the sampled narrative does not appear copied from publicly indexed pages. It does not cover subscription-only full text, private student submissions, or every sentence in all cited papers. The overlaps that did appear during source review were expected: article titles, author names, SPMe and PyBaMM terminology, Chen2020 parameter-set language, spherical diffusion equations, thermal balance, Cheng–Verbrugge stress equations, and standard metric definitions. These expressions should remain accurate and cited. Rewriting a paper title, physical law, symbol definition, or reproducibility value merely to lower a score would weaken the paper rather than improve its originality.

## Earlier submission remains the main similarity source

The supplied Turnitin report concerns the earlier manuscript and reported **18% overall similarity, including 17% attributed to KPR Institute student papers**. That pattern is consistent with the authors' own earlier submission being stored in an institutional repository. Turnitin explicitly describes earlier drafts as a cause of large self-matches and allows an instructor to exclude a previous submission when it is not relevant to the assessment ([Turnitin similarity-score guidance](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/23435833938701-Understanding-the-similarity-score); [filters and exclusions](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/23476785544973-Managing-filters-and-exclusions-in-the-Similarity-Report)). The earlier report is available locally as [A Physics-Consistent Electrochemical.pdf](<C:/Users/naresh v/Downloads/A Physics-Consistent Electrochemical.pdf>).

An independent normalized-token comparison confirms that the new manuscript is substantially rewritten in its narrative sections but still reuses long blocks. At a threshold of 12 consecutive normalized words, matching runs cover **43.34% of the complete revised PDF and 30.46% of the body after the bibliography is removed**. The unchanged bibliography alone accounts for 1,052 matching tokens, or 18.50 percentage points of the full-document coverage. The introduction, related-work narrative, and conclusion have no exact 12-word block from the earlier version, while the governing-physics section is unchanged in this measurement. Table I, the architecture and residual explanations, the experimental configuration, metric descriptions, coupling check, and timing protocol account for most remaining body overlap. Full methods and caveats are documented in the [local overlap audit](../research_notes/PINN%20paper%20originality%20audit/local_overlap.md), with the compared files available as the [revised PDF](../deliverables/PINN_EV_Battery_Authorship_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.pdf) and [earlier PDF](<C:/Users/naresh v/Downloads/PINN_EV_Battery_Originality_Revised_IEEE_8_Pages.pdf>).

These percentages must not be reported as Turnitin similarity. The local method lowercases text, removes punctuation, repairs PDF line-end hyphenation, and measures exact matching sequences; it does not search Turnitin's sources or apply its exclusions. Its value is diagnostic: **a new institutional submission may still show a material self-match unless the earlier KPR paper is excluded or the avoidable repeated explanations are independently redrafted**.

## Style review finds specificity and formulaic compression

The supplied AI report also evaluates the earlier manuscript, not the revised paper; its **34% indicator cannot be transferred to the revision**. The new manuscript was instead reviewed qualitatively. Its strongest passages sound grounded in real project work: the previous network's independent stress heads could conflict with its own concentration field; complete operating conditions are held out to prevent trajectory leakage; radial integration uses an analytic centre limit; mechanics is retained while degradation is postponed because single-discharge data cannot validate aging; and failed residual patterns are tied to specific implementation errors. These details should be preserved because they explain decisions the authors can defend.

The remaining style concern is structural rather than lexical. Several sections repeatedly use balanced lists and a uniform claim–qualification–scope pattern. The abstract compresses baselines, budgets, seeds, residuals, metrics, and exclusions into a short space. The introduction uses a four-part contribution sequence, the literature review sometimes reads like prose generated from Table I, and the metrics section enumerates nine measures in one sentence. Most consequentially, the results table still contains pending values and the discussion states what will be interpreted before the completed runs are available. **Real results and an evidence-led discussion would improve the paper's credibility more than synonym replacement or deliberate sentence distortion.** The review also found a duplicated `Evaluation measures` subsection heading; this has now been corrected in the downloadable PDF and Overleaf source. The full passage-level assessment is in the [qualitative style audit](../research_notes/PINN%20paper%20originality%20audit/ai_style.md).

## Three actions address the real risks

**Priority 1 is to complete the experiment and replace every pending value.** The final discussion should state what actually happened across seeds and held-out conditions, including a failure, tradeoff, or unexpected result if one occurred. No checker can compensate for an empirical paper that still reads as a protocol, and no result should be invented to make the manuscript appear complete.

**Priority 2 is to redraft the avoidable retained prose while preserving technical truth.** The equations, citations, author details, parameter values, and experimental settings should remain intact. The authors should independently rewrite the explanations around the governing equations, hard constraints, metric choices, coupling check, latency protocol, and Table I's distinction column. Each author should be able to explain those paragraphs orally.

**Priority 3 is to request the correct institutional comparison.** The guide or Turnitin administrator should inspect the 17% KPR source and, if it is the authors' permitted earlier draft, exclude that submission. The new report should record whether bibliography, quoted text, cited material, and small matches were excluded; these settings can materially change the displayed score ([Turnitin assignment settings](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/36214251672973-Standard-assignment-settings)). The authors should then review the actual matched passages rather than treating the headline percentage as a plagiarism verdict.

## Conclusion

The independent evidence does not show suspicious copying from sampled public-web sources. It does show substantial, identifiable self-overlap with the earlier institutional manuscript, much of it legitimate technical material and some of it avoidable explanatory prose. The appropriate remedy combines accurate rewriting, completed experimental evidence, and transparent exclusion of the earlier repository copy.

Other apps can add useful checks, but their scores should be treated as screening signals. The only defensible final statement will come from a fresh institutional report interpreted with its source list and settings, alongside the authors' ability to explain the model, data, equations, and results in their own words.
